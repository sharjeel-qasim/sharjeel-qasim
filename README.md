### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Senior Software Development Engineer & Cloud Architect** with over 7 years of industry experience designing and shipping high-performance distributed systems, resilient microservices, and enterprise web platforms. Currently pursuing a **Master of Science in Applied Artificial Intelligence** at **Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt (THI)** in Germany, I bridge the gap between robust backend engineering and cutting-edge AI technologies.

- 🔭 **Current Focus**: Building scalable cloud architectures with **.NET 8/9**, **Azure**, and **Redis**, while integrating Generative AI & Machine Learning pipelines.
- 🎓 **Education**: M.Sc. in Applied Artificial Intelligence — *Technische Hochschule Ingolstadt (THI), Germany* 🇩🇪
- 💼 **Industry Background**: Senior Software Development Engineer at **A3E Systems**; extensive track record in architecting enterprise solutions, event-driven pipelines, and high-throughput APIs.
- ⚡ **Engineering Philosophy**: Clean Architecture, Domain-Driven Design (DDD), strict asynchronous programming, zero-downtime scalability, and test-driven reliability.
- 📍 **Location**: Ingolstadt, Bavaria, Germany
